# Rita Roncassaglia — Italian Ancestry & Heritage Journeys

A showcase website for **Rita Roncassaglia**, who helps American families
rediscover their Italian origins — researching family histories in Italian
archives, locating the villages their ancestors came from, and (when everyone
agrees) reconnecting them with the relatives who still live there.

---

## The purpose

Between the late 1800s and early 1900s, millions of Italians emigrated to the
United States. Generations later, their descendants often know they are
"Italian" — but not *how*, or *where from*.

This site presents Rita's service for those families: she does the on-the-ground
research that does not exist online, finds the ancestral town, and — for families
who want to travel — accompanies them through Italy in person.

Because every family's story is different, the site lists **no fixed prices**.
Instead, it invites each visitor to request a **personalised quote**, or to write to
Rita and arrange a free introductory call.

## What's on the site

| Page | What it covers |
|------|----------------|
| **Home** | Introduction to Rita and what she offers |
| **About Rita** | Who she is and how she works |
| **Services** | Two ways to work together — the *Research Package* (research done for you, with a written report) and the *Full Heritage Journey* (Rita travels with you in Italy) |
| **The Experience** | Step-by-step of how a journey unfolds, plus FAQ |
| **Stories** | The families who have already travelled with Rita, in their own words |
| **Request a quote** | A short, guided form that sends Rita a tailored request |
| **Contact** | Email, and the pathway for visitors who don't have a family tree yet |
| **Privacy · Cookies** | What the site does with data. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics. |

---

## The site in short

- A warm, editorial design in an Italian heritage palette (terracotta, cream,
  olive, wine, gold).
- Real photographs from the journeys, in hand-drawn frames, on *The Experience*;
  custom illustrations of the Italian countryside elsewhere.
- Fully responsive (phone, tablet, desktop) with an optional dark mode.
- A quote request form that emails Rita the visitor's preferences.
- Self-hosted typefaces, no analytics, no cookies, no third-party embeds.

## Viewing the site

It is a plain static website — HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, with no build
step. Open `index.html` directly in a browser, or serve the folder:

```bash
python -m http.server 5510
```

Then open <http://localhost:5510>.
