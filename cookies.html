Cookies

There is no cookie banner, because there are no cookies.

Two small preferences live in your browser and never leave it. This page says exactly which, and how to get rid of them.

This site sets no cookies

Not one. There is no analytics, no advertising network, no social media button, no embedded video or map, and no consent banner — because there is nothing to consent to. The typefaces are served from this site rather than from Google, so no request leaves your browser simply because you opened a page.

What is stored on your device

Two small things live in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device, they are not sent to us, and they are not readable by anyone else.

  • rr_theme — whether you chose the light or the dark version of the site, so it looks the same next time you visit.
  • rr_last_submit_at — the moment you last sent a quote request. It exists so that an automated script cannot fire the form dozens of times in a row.

None of these identify you, follow you across other websites, or feed any kind of profile.

How to remove them

Clearing the site data for this website in your browser's settings removes both at once. In Chrome, Edge and Firefox it is under Privacy → Cookies and site data; in Safari, under Settings → Privacy → Manage website data. Nothing on the site stops working afterwards — you will just be asked for your light or dark preference again.

When you send us a quote request

At that moment, and only at that moment, your answers are sent to Formspree, which delivers them to Rita's mailbox. What happens to them afterwards is described in the privacy notice.

If this ever changes

If a cookie is ever added to this site — for a statistics tool, for instance — this page will say so before it happens, and a proper consent banner will appear.
