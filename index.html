What we do

Italian roots, found and walked

We work with American families who already have a family tree and want the next chapter: the actual paper trail, the home town, and — if your relatives in Italy agree — the reunion itself.

Document research

We visit Italian municipal archives, parish registers and state archives in person — the records that don't live online — to verify your tree branch by branch.

Ancestral villages

We locate the birthplaces of your ancestors and confirm what still stands — the house, the church, the cemetery, the road that led them to the ship.

Living relatives

If we find descendants of your family still living in Italy, we approach them with care and only with their consent do we put you in touch — or arrange a visit.

The real off-path Italian lifestyle

Rita can accompany you through the regions, drive when needed, translate at the meetings, and point you to the food & wine worth your time — anywhere in Italy.

Who is Rita

About Rita

Graduated from Bologna University in 1995 with a major in Linguistics – English Language and Literature, I have lived, worked and travelled between USA and Italy for thirty years.

The best part of my job is helping people from all over the world to build connections and establish relationships as they discover and fall in love with Italy. My goal is to assist you in finding your family's hometown, learning about your Italian ancestors and whenever possible reconnect with your living relatives.

The Research Package

For families who want answers, from afar.

  • Verification of your existing family tree against Italian records
  • On-site visits to the relevant municipalities and parishes
  • Photographs of the ancestral house, street, church
  • Discreet search for descendants still living in Italy
  • Written report — what we found, what we couldn't find, and why
  • Rita does the research on her own; you receive the result
