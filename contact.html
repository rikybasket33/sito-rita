I don't have a family tree yet — can you help?

We strongly suggest you arrive with a family tree already built. The research is faster, deeper and less expensive when we start from a clear starting point.

That said: if you haven't built one yet, we can suggest you to start one on FamilySearch.org, which is free and is where most families begin. Tell us the names, towns and dates you already have, and we will point you to where the American side of the search starts — passenger manifests, naturalisation records, census data, and the memories of the older people in your family.

Once your tree is established on the American side, we take over on the Italian side. Tell us what you have and what you need.
