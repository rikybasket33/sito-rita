Before you start: what you are quoting

The quote covers Rita's work only — the archive research, the documents, the contact with your relatives, and her being beside you to translate. Her fee depends on how much research your family needs, which is why there is no price list.

Flights, hotels, restaurants, car hire and experiences are not part of the fee: they are arranged separately and stay in your name. Rita is glad to point you towards what is worth your time, and she takes no commission from anyone she recommends. Travel and archive fees for the research are reimbursed at cost, with receipts. More on what's included →

If you would like Rita to drive your rental car, she goes on the rental agreement as a named driver — as the main driver, or alongside you, whichever suits the way you book.
